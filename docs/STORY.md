# Conversation and alchemical-core quest

Mother's full-screen conversation is drawn at native screen resolution by `conversation.gd`, independently of the pixelated 3D world. It uses transparent Mother/hero/villager cutouts in `game/assets/conversation/`, over the current game viewport blurred by `frosted_backdrop.gd`. Original source artwork remains available as art references, not alternative runtime implementations. Complete built-in image-generation prompts are saved beside each cutout. Choices support mouse/keyboard focus; E/Escape leave. Conversation/inventory/help/death pause the player, enemies and projectiles. Area changes clear projectiles.

Talk to Mother downstairs inside the coastal west cottage and choose the forest errand. Defeat the slime to unlock the south forest exit, then follow the old cobbled road into Warden's Hollow past two briar boars and a stone warden. Loot the calcified aether core from the defeated warden's collapsed remains with E. Return it to Mother to complete the quest and recover health/stamina. The HUD tracks the next objective.

The two boars share pack activation, telegraph together and charge toward the player from separate positions. The dense forest has a winding, moss-covered cobbled route with missing and uneven pavers, continuing through both areas to the end. The warden's armor blocks sword/water damage until a thrown rock is parried back. Face the rock and right-click just before impact: reflection deals 18 damage and opens a five-second damage window with cyan glow. Missed rocks deal 18 damage. Stone flight checks walls. Throws have 1.15-second wind-up and 2.3-second recovery.

Placements are in map definitions; tuning is `forest_enemies.json`; AI/geometry is `forest_enemy.gd`. Permanent defeat is shared through `slime.gd`. No enemy automatically respawns; `respawn()` remains an explicit test/reset helper. F5/Save persists quest stage and four stable defeated IDs. Legacy saves default to an unstarted quest. Saved legacy wisp defeat migrates to the second boar so completed quests stay valid. Unknown stages, IDs, duplicates and impossible completion states are rejected.

At zero health, the death overlay waits for **Wake in the village square**. Respawn restores health/stamina, sheathes the sword, and retains the recovered core/quest/defeats. Save explicitly before closing; tests never overwrite the player's save.

Use `tools/platform.cmd capture --view quest` and `capture-windows --view quest` for rendered conversation, villager dialogue, inventory equip/unequip, reveal, enemies, thrown rock, death and village-respawn views.

Inventory opens with I or its menu button. Weapon, body, feet and charm slots display equipment; the weapon slot and carried sword toggle actual sword ownership/equipment. Starter dress and boots occupy their slots; Windstep Charm is available through the northern goblin campaign; its slot toggles the movement boost. Sword equipment state is saved and old saves default to equipped. The high-quality empty-handed heroine illustration is shared with conversation mode.

The corpse remains on map return and after saving; its glowing core disappears once looted. component_ready replaces the former herb possession stage; legacy herbs_ready saves migrate without losing quest progress. A core requires a defeated warden, not all optional encounters. Map IDs stay unchanged for save compatibility.

The Village Green keeper gives a one-time wishing coin through dialogue. Interact with the existing well and choose to throw it in: the coin becomes an Everfull Experience Potion. Inventory [I] offers repeatable +1,000 XP drinks; the bottle is never consumed. The current tree tops out at level 15; XP remains saveable at the existing 100,000 limit. Skills still require point allocation and root prerequisites. Save field well_gift_stage is not_started / coin_ready / potion_owned, defaulting to not_started for old saves. Respawning retains the gift. Verify/capture with potion_checks.gd and capture-windows --view potion.
