# World Rise camera perspectives

C switches between first and third person in World Rise. First person remains the default on a fresh installation. The choice is stored in `user://world-rise-camera.cfg`, independently of offline character saves and portable online characters. Existing strict save camera values remain compatible; third-person saves serialize the existing first-person value and restore the local preference.

Both perspectives retain `follow_camera.gd` as the Actor's canonical view driver. Mouse yaw/pitch, movement, animated head frame, action clocks, steel trajectories, bow nock, launch clearance and ballistic preview have one owner. The third-person camera never supplies combat transforms. Its reticle projects the canonical aim ray into the displayed camera, including animation/recoil and world obstruction. The bow retains its gravity-bearing physical trajectory preview.

`wr_third_person_camera.gd` owns a presentation-only 3.8m boom with a 22cm swept sphere, starting-overlap protection, immediate obstruction contraction and eased outward recovery. Terrain and scenery remain solid. A tight obstruction falls back to the canonical eye camera and existing first-person arms, with the local body hidden to keep the head out of the near plane. The regular shoulder is right of the character; the bow uses the left shoulder so the real left-hand bow and nock remain visible. Mouse/pointer, overlay and focus handling remain shared with first person.

The existing native heroine and weapon art supply the body and steel. `beta_hands.gd` fits the greatsword's body hands to the actual hilt, including bounded shoulder protraction without bone scaling. The existing weapon runtime fits spear, rapier and bow hands. No art source or animation is regenerated, no independent third-person attack trajectories are introduced, and weapon reach/resources remain Actor-owned.

Focused regression fixture: `res://scripts/wr_third_person_checks.gd`, run with disposable user data through the platform tool's `run_process(..., isolated=True)`. Native images are opt-in with `--camera-capture-dir=/absolute/path`; the default fixture supports headless runs. The side-angle images are diagnostic camera views of the same physical pose, not alternate combat frames. Existing `wr_client_hurt_checks.gd` covers actual Main/prediction self exclusions and remote hurt volumes. Historical `camera_checks.gd` describes the superseded Follow/First/Overhead gameplay cycle and is not acceptance evidence for this toggle.
