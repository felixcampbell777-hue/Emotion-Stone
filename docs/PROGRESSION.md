# Strength, Form and Mana

P or Inventory > Level Up opens three upward hexagonal trees over the blurred live game. Inventory > Skills equips unlocked heavy and water attacks; Equipment & Items handles the sword and potion, and Quests shows current objectives. Select a hexagon to read its effect, then invest one point. Both paths of every split can be learned; each costs one point. Roots need only one rank before their split. Strength and Form may instead keep stacking their roots to five ranks, including after choosing a path. F5 saves allocations and equipment. Later continuation nodes are placeholders with no behavior.

| Branch | Root | First split: both available |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strength | Each rank: +4 sword damage, +10 maximum HP; 5 ranks maximum | Measured Force: 30% less swing stamina **and** Crushing Force: 50% more sword damage |
| Form | Each rank: +5% walk/sprint speed, 8% less sprint stamina; 5 ranks maximum | Driving Thrust: inventory-equipped heavy charge with sliding recovery **and** Dodge: Ctrl / Shift tap with stand-up |
| Mana | Unlock Water Slash | Water Lance unlock **and** Flowing Splash: 60% less slash stamina (24 to 9.6) |

Bonus damage is added before Crushing Force's multiplier. Swing savings cover all light-combo stages, overhead heavy and thrust, not parry or water. Form speed applies to sheathed/drawn walking and sprinting; sprint savings cover both equipment states. Roots never heal or refill on investment. Stats rebuild from combat.json before applying ranks so refresh/load cannot multiply them again.

Water skills are unavailable until learned. Inventory selects unlocked Q specials and the heavy style; default overhead stays available. Driving Thrust replaces held-left-click/K heavy attacks when equipped. The other Form path rolls on a Shift press/release within .30 seconds; a longer hold sprints. Roll costs 18 stamina, has a .85-second tucked forward somersault, can be damaged throughout, followed by a vulnerable .55-second stand-up. The sword follows the same root transformation as the body. Sheathed/unequipped dodges restore their previous stance. See COMBAT.md for timings and motion contracts.

Every actual creature kill grants a fixed reward. Story slime / boar / warden pay 35 / 60 / 120 XP; their existing one-time defeats remain permanent. Menagery creatures and custom-world placements pay their catalog style's reward: moss slime 35, resin slime 45, bramble slime 50, briar piglet 55, sapwing bat 20, thorn hare 30, acorn squirrel 35 and puffcap 45 XP. Fresh Menagery spawns can be defeated repeatedly to grind; replacing or clearing a living specimen pays nothing, and hitting an already dead specimen cannot pay twice. Custom creatures retain one-time world defeat records.

Mother's core turn-in pays 80 XP once; finishing the wishing-well quest pays 40 once. Existing early thresholds are preserved so old earned levels/allocations remain valid. Levels now extend to 50; from level 15 onward each next level's cost rises by 14% (rounded to a whole XP). Every level costs strictly more XP than the previous level. One skill point per level after level 1. All 17 possible ranks/unlocks are valid at higher levels. XP can safely accumulate up to 10,000,000. Death retains XP/ranks.
