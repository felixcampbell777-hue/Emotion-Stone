# Beta shirt back coverage — v0.27.1

The separate editable Beta heroine now has a continuous denim panel covering the upper back and rear/side shoulder seams, from inside the belt up beneath the collar and short sleeves. The former skin patches came from different shirt/body deformation around the shoulder joints. The new surface copies the actual body weights. Skin faces directly covered by the panel and overlapping old rear cloth triangles are masked from this derived presentation, so they cannot flash through during arm motion. The neck, short sleeve length and original visible skin outside the garment remain.

The panel reuses the existing trusted CC0 Poly Haven Denim Fabric 05 maps, with albedo matched to the current baked outfit and a continuous planar weave UV. This avoids crossing unrelated islands in the old cloth atlas. The original source material files remain unchanged. Initial repair recipes: cover_beta_shirt_back.py, tidy_beta_shirt_seams.py, finish_beta_shirt_material.py. These are initial art steps, not the export command for later manual edits.

Editable source: art/source/beta-heroine.blend. tools/cover_beta_shirt_back.py modifies only that current derived source; no personal or canonical master is opened for writing. tools/export_beta_saved.py reopens/export this saved source, retaining its sixteen bones, nineteen actions and existing PBR maps. Do not rerun the personal-input baker export_beta_heroine.py to export current hand edits. The saved-source/runtime hashes are in art/character/beta-heroine-export.json; coverage bounds, weights and reference samples are in art/character/shirt-review/coverage.json. No extra asset download was needed.
